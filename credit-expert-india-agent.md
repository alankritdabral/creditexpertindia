# Credit Expert India — Landing Page Agent Brief

## 1. Project Overview

Build a modern, trustworthy, conversion-focused landing page for **Credit Expert India**, an Indian credit and loan assistance business.

The business helps customers who are struggling with expensive or multiple debts and also helps eligible customers find personal loans and fresh loan options through its network of banking/lending partners.

The website should feel **professional, trustworthy, premium, human, and financially credible** — not like a spammy loan-lead website.

Primary objective:
- Generate qualified leads.
- Encourage visitors to check eligibility.
- Encourage visitors to speak with a credit expert.
- Explain debt consolidation and loan solutions clearly.
- Build trust before asking for personal information.

---

## 2. Core Positioning

### Primary positioning

> **Paying Too Much Interest on Your Loans? Let's Find a Better Way Forward.**

Credit Expert India helps borrowers explore suitable options for:
- High-interest existing loans
- Credit-card outstanding
- Multiple EMIs
- App/fintech loans
- Loan balance transfers
- Debt consolidation
- Fresh personal loans

Do NOT position the company as simply another generic loan marketplace.

The strongest differentiator is:

> **Helping people understand and potentially reduce the burden of expensive existing debt.**

---

## 3. Services

### A. Debt Consolidation

Target customers:
- Multiple personal loans
- Multiple EMIs
- Credit-card outstanding
- High-interest loans
- App/fintech loans
- High monthly EMI burden

Messaging:

> **Turn Multiple EMIs Into One More Manageable Repayment**

Explain that eligible existing debts may potentially be consolidated/refinanced into a suitable loan structure.

Avoid guaranteeing savings or approval.

---

### B. Personal Loans

For eligible salaried customers.

Current business claim:

> **Personal loan interest rates as low as 9.95% p.a.* for eligible applicants.**

The `*` must lead to clear qualification language.

Rates depend on factors such as:
- Credit profile
- Income
- Employer
- Existing obligations
- Loan amount
- Tenure
- Lender policy

Do not state that every customer will receive 9.95%.

---

### C. Loan Balance Transfer / Interest Reduction

Help customers explore whether their existing loan can be transferred/refinanced to potentially obtain better terms.

Current business claim:

> **Help customers reduce their interest burden, potentially to rates around 11% or lower where eligible.**

Do not present 11% as a guaranteed rate.

---

### D. Fresh Loans

Provide eligible customers with access to fresh personal-loan options.

Messaging:
- Fast service
- Multiple lending options
- Expert assistance
- Simple process
- Support through documentation and processing

Do not promise guaranteed approval or guaranteed disbursal time.

### 03 — Review Your Profile

### 04 — Explore Suitable Options
We help identify options from applicable banking/lending partners.

### 05 — Complete Documentation
Submit the documents required by the relevant lender.

- Guaranteed approval
- Guaranteed disbursal

# 24. Design Direction

The design should feel:

- Premium
- Financial
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Clean
- Human
- Indian but not stereotypical

Avoid:
- Cheap stock imagery
- Excessive gradients
- Flashy animations
- Fake "approved" badges
- Red warning-heavy designs
- Casino-like loan advertising
- Excessive popups
- Aggressive countdown timers
- "100% guaranteed" messaging

Preferred visual language:
- Deep navy / dark blue foundation
- White backgrounds
- Subtle green/teal accent for positive financial outcomes
- Strong typography
- Large whitespace
- Clean cards
- Subtle shadows
- Professional financial illustrations
- Real people only where authentic

The design should look closer to a modern fintech/financial advisory company than a traditional loan broker.

# 28. Compliance / Claims Rules

This is a financial-services website. Be conservative with claims.

Never state as guaranteed:

- Loan approval
- Interest rate
- EMI reduction
- Savings
- Disbursal time
- Credit score improvement
- Lowest rate
- Guaranteed eligibility

Use:

- "starting from"
- "as low as"
- "eligible applicants"
- "subject to lender criteria"
- "may"
- "potentially"
- "indicative"
- "illustrative"

Any financial claims, lender relationships, regulatory statements, rates, fees and legal disclosures must be verified before production.

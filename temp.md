Clear Messaging: Prioritize a single, concise headline and sub-headline that immediately explain the problem you solve and the value you provide 
Purposeful Animation: Use motion to guide the user’s eye to the most important parts of the page, rather than using it as a distraction 
Visual Hierarchy: Avoid placing multiple, equal-weight headlines or too many calls-to-action (CTAs) above the fold; create a clear focal point 
Simplicity and Clarity: Avoid jargon and overly technical language that alienates potential customers; use simple, readable text 
Optimized Performance: Ensure images and media are optimized so the site loads quickly, especially for mobile users, as heavy pages negatively impact SEO and bounce rates 
Design Consistency: Keep layout clean and trustworthy, and ensure text is easy to read (e.g., avoid light grey text on dark backgrounds)
